Subsidy eligible

On-grid system

Panels feed your home first. Extra power goes to the grid through a net meter and is adjusted against your bill.

  • Lowest cost per kW and the fastest payback
  • Qualifies for PM Surya Ghar central subsidy
  • Shuts off during a power cut, as grid safety rules require
Subsidy depends on setup

Hybrid system with battery

Works like on-grid, plus a battery that keeps lights, fans and the fridge running when power goes.

  • Backup during outages
  • Higher upfront cost, and batteries need replacing over the system's life
  • We confirm subsidy eligibility for your configuration before you commit

The central subsidy, and what we file for you.

The subsidy is paid by the Government of India straight into your bank account after the system is installed, inspected and commissioned.

System sizeCentral subsidySuits homes using
1 kW₹30,000up to ~300 units / 2 months
2 kW₹60,000~300–500 units / 2 months
3 kW and above₹78,000~500+ units / 2 months (cap)

Residential rates as published for the scheme: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000. Rates can change. Check the official portal at pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

Keep these ready

  • Latest TNEB electricity bill (service connection number)
  • Aadhaar card of the connection holder
  • Property tax receipt or other ownership proof
  • Bank passbook or cancelled cheque for the subsidy transfer
  • Passport-size photo

The connection must be in your name, and the subsidy is for one residential connection per household.

Questions homeowners ask

Frequently asked questions

What size system do I need?

Look at the units on your last two or three TNEB bills. Each 1 kW makes about 250 units every two months in Chennai. A home using 600 units per bill usually needs about 2.5 kW. Use the calculator above for a quick estimate, and we confirm it at the site visit.

Will solar work during a power cut?

An on-grid system switches off when the grid goes down, so line workers stay safe. If you want backup during cuts, choose a hybrid system with a battery.

How does net metering work in Tamil Nadu?

A bi-directional meter records power you draw from the grid and power your panels send back. You pay for the difference. Homes up to 10 kW can use net metering. Surplus credit is settled by TNEB at a rate lower than what you pay per unit, so it pays to size the system to your own use.

Is my terrace suitable?

You need about 100 sq ft of shadow-free space per kW. Flat RCC roofs are ideal. Water tanks, staircases and nearby trees cast shadows, so we map them before designing. Sheet and tiled roofs can work with a different mounting structure.

I live in an apartment. Can I get solar?

Individual subsidy applies to independent homes with their own roof. Apartments can install on the common terrace through the association, which falls under a different subsidy category. Talk to us with your association's details.

How long until the subsidy arrives?

The subsidy is released after the net meter is installed and the system is commissioned on the portal. That is typically about a month after commissioning, but timing depends on government processing.
